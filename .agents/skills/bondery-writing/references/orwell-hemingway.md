# Orwell-Hemingway

Craft for published user-facing English. Personality stays in [`bondery-brand`](../../bondery-brand/SKILL.md). Product microcopy mechanics stay in [`bondery-ux`](../../bondery-ux/references/common/ux-writing.md).

Do not apply this register to agent replies, plans, comments, or tech specs. Those use [asd-100.md](./asd-100.md).

## Orwell's six rules

From *Politics and the English Language*. Apply them in this order:

1. Never use a metaphor, simile, or other figure of speech which you are used to seeing in print.
2. Never use a long word where a short one will do.
3. If it is possible to cut a word out, always cut it out.
4. Never use the passive where you can use the active.
5. Never use a foreign phrase, a scientific word, or a jargon word if you can think of an everyday English equivalent.
6. Break any of these rules sooner than say anything outright barbarous.

## Hemingway craft

- Short sentences. Prefer a period to a stack of commas.
- One idea per sentence.
- Concrete nouns and verbs. Name the thing.
- Cut weakening adverbs (`very`, `really`, `actually`, `just`, `simply`).
- No throat-clearing (`It is important to note`, `In order to`, `We are excited to`).
- Specifics over claims. A number or an example beats "powerful" or "seamless".

## Compose with brand

Warmth is not extra adjectives. Warmth is a concrete human detail.

This register must not turn landing copy into a war dispatch. Keep Bondery's warm-friend voice from `bondery-brand`. Cut fluff. Keep the person.

| Cold (too much Hemingway) | Warm and still tight |
|---------------------------|----------------------|
| Store contacts. Open them later. | Keep the people who matter, and know when to reach out. |
| Reduce relationship decay. | It is easy to lose touch. Bondery helps you not to. |

## Before / after

**After (Orwell + Hemingway):**

> When you meet someone, Bondery remembers. A note, a birthday, the last time you talked. Then it nudges you to reach out — not with guilt, with context.

**Before:**

> We are thrilled to announce a seamlessly empowered personal CRM experience that leverages intelligent insights in order to help users proactively optimize their networking workflows.

## Do not apply

- Legal and privacy claims — [`bondery-legal`](../../bondery-legal/SKILL.md)
- Code, identifiers, error codes, paths, env vars
- Quoted user text
- Agent replies, plans, comments, JSDoc, API/deploy/contributing docs — [asd-100.md](./asd-100.md)

## Checklist

- [ ] Stock metaphors, jargon, and filler are gone
- [ ] Short words, active voice, cut-able words cut
- [ ] Sentences are short and concrete
- [ ] Warmth comes from detail, not adjectives
- [ ] Brand personality still reads as Bondery, not a spec
