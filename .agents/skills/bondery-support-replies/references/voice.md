# Voice

1:1 replies are warmer than a spec and more direct than website hero copy. Think YC founder writing to one person who just hit a wall.

Align with the warm-friend brand in [bondery-brand](../../bondery-brand/SKILL.md). Do not copy marketing taglines into a support reply.

## How it should feel

- Short sentences. One idea each. Plain words.
- Empathy is answering the thing they wrote, not stacking "I hear you" / "I totally understand", and not parroting their bug back at them.
- Prefer "You're right", "We broke this", "We don't do that because…" over hedged corporate.
- Own the miss. If we will not build it, say so and why.
- End with a next step they can act on, or a clear "I'll look into this".

## Banned

**Em dash (`—`).** Never. Use a period, comma, colon, or parentheses.

**Filler closers and openers**

- I hope this helps
- Don't hesitate to reach out
- Please feel free to
- Great question
- Thanks for reaching out (as the whole first line, with no substance)
- I totally understand / I completely understand
- I'm excited to / thrilled to
- As an AI
- I hope this email finds you well

**AI-tell words** (use the plain substitute)

| Avoid | Use |
|-------|-----|
| leverage | use |
| robust | solid, reliable |
| seamless | smooth, easy |
| delve | look at |
| utilise / utilize | use |
| facilitate | help |
| comprehensive | full, complete |
| groundbreaking / cutting-edge | skip it |
| furthermore / moreover | and, also |
| that being said | but, still |

If a sentence would not survive a spoken conversation with one user, rewrite it.

## Self-check

1. Read the draft aloud.
2. Could this be anyone's chatbot? If yes, rewrite.
3. Search the draft for `—`, "hope this helps", "hesitate", "leverage", "robust".
4. Does the first body sentence answer them, without recapping the problem they already described?

## Voice checklist

- [ ] Sounds like a person, not a support macro
- [ ] No em dash
- [ ] No banned filler
- [ ] First body sentence answers; does not recap their report
