## What you can ask

| You want to… | Example |
|---|---|
| Prepare for a meeting | “Brief me on Alex before our 3pm.” |
| Find someone | “Who did I meet at the Berlin conference named Priya?” |
| Stay in touch | “Who is overdue for keep-in-touch this week?” |
| Capture after a conversation | “Save that I had coffee with Jordan today.” |
| Organize | “Add Priya to Investors and tag her as a founder.” |

Read-only access covers search and lookup. Creating, updating, or deleting records needs **Full access** on the consent screen. Claude asks before it creates, updates, or deletes. Deletes and sending a contact share email ask you to confirm again.

## What it will not do

It is not a replacement for the [web app](./webapp.mdx) or the [REST API](./api.mdx). Use it when you already live in an assistant and want Bondery on tap.

The assistant cannot dump your full address book, merge duplicates, import LinkedIn or Instagram archives, change photos, or manage billing. Those stay in the Bondery app or the [REST API](./api.mdx).

## How to connect

### Claude

Claude's directory carries Bondery as a plugin. Install it, then connect and sign in:

1. Install the **Bondery** plugin in Claude.
2. Open **Connectors**.
3. Choose **Connect** on the Bondery connector.
4. Choose **Use Claude's published identity**.
5. Sign in to your Bondery account.
6. Approve **Read only** or **Full access**.

Install does not sign you in. **Register automatically** cannot sign in either. If Claude asks again after the connect step, sign in again.

On a Team or Enterprise plan, an Owner adds the connector once. Each member then signs in with their own account.

The plugin adds five jobs: catch up before a meeting, get to know someone, save a conversation, find who to reach out to, and share a contact. An onboarding skill introduces Bondery and those jobs. Catch up, get to know, and reach out only read. Saving and sharing write, and they need **Full access**.

The Claude package and the OpenAI package are both generated from `llm-plugins/src`.

### Any MCP client

1. In Bondery, open **Settings → AI assistants**
2. Copy the **MCP URL** (or the **mcp.json** snippet)
3. Add it in Claude Desktop or another MCP client that supports HTTP + OAuth with Client ID Metadata Documents (CIMD)
4. Sign in with your Bondery account and choose **Read only** or **Full access**

On hosted Bondery the URL is `https://api.usebondery.com/mcp`. Self-hosters use `{apiBaseUrl}/mcp` from the same Settings card, as a custom connector in Claude or an entry in any other MCP client. The hosted server is listed in the [MCP Registry](https://registry.modelcontextprotocol.io) as `com.usebondery/mcp`. The Claude directory is a different directory.

Assistants authenticate with **OAuth**, not API keys. Setup details, the tool list, and client notes: [Connect AI assistants (MCP)](../bondery/mcp.mdx).
