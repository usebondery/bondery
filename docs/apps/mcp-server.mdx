The Bondery **MCP server** is how AI assistants work in your address book. You stay in Claude or Cursor; they search people you know, summarize history, and — if you allow it — save new contacts and interactions.

It is not a replacement for the [web app](./webapp.mdx) or the [REST API](./api.mdx). Use it when you already live in an assistant and want Bondery on tap.

## What you can ask

| You want to… | Example |
|---|---|
| Prepare for a meeting | “Brief me on Alex before our 3pm.” |
| Find someone | “Who did I meet at the Berlin conference named Priya?” |
| Stay in touch | “Who is overdue for keep-in-touch this week?” |
| Capture after a conversation | “Save that I had coffee with Jordan today.” |
| Organize | “Add Priya to Investors and tag her as a founder.” |

Read-only access covers search and lookup. Creating, updating, or deleting records needs **Full access** on the consent screen. Deletes and sending a contact share email ask you to confirm in the assistant.

## What it will not do

The assistant cannot dump your full address book, merge duplicates, import LinkedIn or Instagram archives, change photos, or manage billing. Those stay in the Bondery app or the [REST API](./api.mdx).

## How to connect

1. In Bondery, open **Settings → AI assistants**
2. Copy the **MCP URL** (or the **mcp.json** snippet for Cursor)
3. Add it in Claude Desktop, Cursor, or another MCP client that supports HTTP + OAuth
4. Sign in with your Bondery account and choose **Read only** or **Full access**

On hosted Bondery the URL is `https://api.usebondery.com/mcp`. Self-hosters use `{apiBaseUrl}/mcp` from the same Settings card. The hosted server is listed in the [MCP Registry](https://registry.modelcontextprotocol.io) as `com.usebondery/mcp`.

Assistants authenticate with **OAuth**, not API keys. Setup details, the tool list, and client notes: [Connect AI assistants (MCP)](../bondery/mcp.mdx).
