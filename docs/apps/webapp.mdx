The Bondery web app is the primary way to manage your network — contacts, groups, interactions, reminders, and more.

## Access

Open [app.usebondery.com](https://app.usebondery.com) and sign in with **email**, **GitHub**, **LinkedIn**, or a **passkey**. There is no email-and-password login.

## What you can do

| Area | Description |
|---|---|
| **People** | Rich contact records with notes, social links, phones, and addresses |
| **Groups & tags** | Organize contacts into segments that fit your workflow |
| **Interactions** | Log calls, meetings, and messages on a per-person timeline |
| **Reminders** | Birthdays, anniversaries, and custom dates with email notifications |
| **Map** | See where your contacts are located |
| **Import** | Bulk import from LinkedIn or Instagram exports — see [Import](../bondery/import.mdx) |
| **Settings** | API keys, theme, language, and account preferences |
