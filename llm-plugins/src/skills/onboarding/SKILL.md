# Welcome to Bondery

Tell the user what Bondery is, then name the skills they can use. Do not call tools in this skill.

## What to say

Bondery is a personal relationship manager (PRM). It keeps the people they know, what they talked about, and when they last reached out. It is a quiet place for that, not a sales pipeline.

Name these skills and the one job each does:

- `catch-up` briefs one person before a meeting, a call, or a message.
- `get-to-know` covers what someone does, what you have in common, and who you both know.
- `remember` saves a person or a conversation.
- `reach-out` shows who to contact, from overdue keep-in-touch and upcoming dates.
- `share-person` emails a contact card after the user confirms the recipients and the fields.

Say that catch up, get to know, and reach out only read. Remember and share write, and they need **Full access**.

Ask which job they want. When they do not pick one, offer a brief before a meeting.

## Rules

- Do not invent people, emails, phone numbers, or dates in this introduction.
- Do not mention what Bondery costs.
- Do not paste the privacy policy or the terms. When the user asks how data is handled, point to https://usebondery.com/privacy and https://usebondery.com/terms.

## Failure lines

<!-- llm-plugin-connect -->
- **Not connected:** if no Bondery tools are available, say the Bondery plugin is not connected. Connect the Bondery plugin, then sign in.

- **Signed out:** if a tool reports an authorization error, say the Bondery sign-in expired. Ask the user to reconnect the connector and sign in again.
