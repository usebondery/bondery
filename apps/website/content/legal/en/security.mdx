This page explains how to report a security issue in Bondery.

---

## 1. How to report [#report]

Email [team@usebondery.com](mailto:team@usebondery.com) with a description of the issue and the steps to reproduce it.

---

## 2. Encryption [#encryption]

You can encrypt your report or email with our [PGP public key](/.well-known/pgp-key.txt).

---

## 3. Privacy [#privacy]

How we handle personal data is described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
